FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know.

Clear answers about operation, climate requirements, filtration, and warranties.

Output depends on ambient humidity and temperature. Under average indoor conditions (50-70% relative humidity, 70-85°F), the residential countertop/freestanding unit produces between 4 to 8 gallons (15 to 30 liters) of pure water daily—plenty for a family or office break room.
Our systems operate effectively between 30% and 95% relative humidity. In very dry desert conditions (below 25-30% humidity), output decreases, while in typical humid climates and coastal areas, production runs at maximum peak efficiency.
Sound levels range between 42 and 48 dB during active cooling, similar to a quiet household refrigerator or premium air purifier. Once the internal holding tank is full, the unit automatically switches into silent standby mode.
The unit operates on standard 110V/120V household outlets (consuming ~350-450W during active condensation). Average cost of electrical power per gallon produced is approximately $0.08 to $0.12—a 90%+ discount compared to purchasing bottled water.
Like any refrigeration system, the compressor exhausts mildly warm air from the back vents while extracting moisture. In rooms with ordinary ventilation or air conditioning, temperature variation is barely noticeable.
The external HEPA air mesh is easily rinsed under a faucet every 2-3 months. Internal carbon block and remineralization cartridges last 6 to 12 months, and the UV-C LED sterilization lamp is rated for 30,000+ operating hours.
Every unit includes a comprehensive 1-Year Manufacturer Warranty covering parts and labor, plus a 30-Day In-Home Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with water purity and performance, contact us for an easy return.
Exceptionally crisp, sweet, and pure. Unlike distilled or reverse osmosis water which tastes flat and acidic, our remineralization cartridge infuses natural calcium and magnesium ions to yield an ideal alkaline pH of 7.2 to 7.6.
