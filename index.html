HOW IT WORKS

From Air
to Water.
Simply.

Our multi-stage process captures humidity from the air, purifies it, and delivers clean, mineral-balanced drinking water.

LEARN MORE ABOUT THE PROCESS

1. Air Intake

Ambient air is drawn in through advanced filtration.

2. Condensation

Moisture in the air is condensed into pure water.

3. Filtration

Multi-stage purification ensures clean, safe, great-tasting water.

4. Clean Water

Fresh, mineral-balanced drinking water ready when you are.
